Coronary artery disease

Specialized Heart Center editorial teamPublished: 2026-10-05

Coronary arteries supply the heart muscle. When they narrow or become blocked, effort symptoms or more serious events may follow. This is a general introduction, not a personal risk score.

What the term refers to

Coronary artery disease usually means atherosclerosis or narrowing in these arteries. Confirming it can involve clinical assessment, ECG, stress testing, and specialist imaging that is not all performed in every clinic.

Symptoms that deserve attention

Pressure or pain in the chest linked to effort, new breathlessness, or pain spreading to the arm or jaw in worrying settings. Women and older adults may describe less typical symptoms. Any severe sudden presentation is an emergency.

What this center provides

The Specialized Heart Center lists consultation, echocardiography, ECG, stress ECG, and Holter monitoring. Some coronary imaging or interventional procedures may need another facility, depending on the case. The website does not claim to perform every cardiac intervention on site.

Coronary catheterization

The doctor’s stated specialty includes cardiology and coronary catheterization. What is done inside this clinic versus what is referred to a hospital is discussed in the consultation, not inferred from a general page.

If emergency symptoms appear — severe chest pain, sudden shortness of breath, fainting, or sudden weakness on one side of the body — seek urgent medical help immediately. In Egypt you can call ambulance services on 123.
