Pre-operative and pre-delivery cardiac assessment

A cardiac opinion before surgery or childbirth helps the treating team understand the heart before the procedure.

About this service

When is it requested?

A surgeon, anesthetist, or obstetrician may ask for a heart assessment before an operation or delivery, especially with age, other illnesses, or cardiac symptoms.

What does the cardiologist do?

The visit reviews history and examination. Echo, ECG, or other tests may be requested before an opinion is written. That opinion supports the treating team; it does not replace the surgical decision itself.

What we do not promise

We do not publish a fixed validity period for every report, or a mandatory lab list for every operation. That depends on the assessment and the referring request.

This service includes

  • Cardiac review before surgery
  • Cardiac review before delivery
  • Tests coordinated according to the case

Bring the referral note and previous reports if available.
