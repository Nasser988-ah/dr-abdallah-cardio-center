Dr. Abdallah Ahmed Abdallah sees patients and families at the Specialized Heart Center in 10th of Ramadan City for heart and vascular assessment and related diagnostic tests.

This profile does not invent extra degrees, hospital posts, or awards. The verified facts here are the stated specialty, the clinic role, and the services the center actually lists.

A visit usually starts with the history and symptoms. Tests are added only when they help: clinical examination, echocardiography, ECG, stress ECG, Holter monitoring, or assessment before surgery or sport.

The clinic sees adults and also offers echocardiography for children and newborns when that is needed.
