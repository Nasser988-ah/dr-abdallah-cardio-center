Frequently asked questions
about booking

These answers cover appointments, hours, and the address. They are not a medical diagnosis and do not replace a consultation.

How do I book?

Call 01099559224 or 01220664555, or send WhatsApp to the primary number. Reception books the visit; there is no online scheduler.

What are the opening hours?

4:00 PM to 8:00 PM, daily except Friday. Please confirm before you arrive.

Where is the clinic?

10th of Ramadan City, Al-Ordonia, Al-Kamal Center, next to Techno Scan Radiology, behind Cinco Mall and the Alexandria Library, above Dr. Nadia Pharmacy.

Who is the doctor?

Dr. Abdallah Ahmed Abdallah, consultant of cardiology and coronary catheterization.

Which services are available?

Heart and vascular consultation, cardiac tests and respiratory assessment, pediatric and newborn echo, stress ECG and Holter monitoring, sports cardiac assessment, and pre-operative or pre-delivery assessment.

Do you offer pediatric echocardiography?

Yes. The clinic provides heart ultrasound for children and newborns. Details are on the dedicated service page.

Are prices listed on the website?

No. Fees are not published here as a single figure for every case. Ask reception when you book.

Are you open on Friday?

No. Friday is outside the published clinic hours.
